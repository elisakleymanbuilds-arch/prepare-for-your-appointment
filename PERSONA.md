# Persona Test Layer 1

## Persona

I am 24 years old and live in Mexico. I received a blood test result that confused me. I have a doctor appointment soon, but I do not know what to ask. I am worried and want to feel more prepared.

## Test Results

### First screen

The user understood the main purpose of the app immediately.

Their first action was to fill out the two fields.

**Confusion:** None.

### After generating questions

The user understood what to do with the generated questions.

They would save the questions, take them to the medical appointment, ask the doctor, and then use the notes section to record the doctor's instructions.

**Confusion:** None.

## Main Finding

The user understood the complete flow without help:

1. Enter the situation.
2. Enter the main concern.
3. Generate questions.
4. Take the questions to the appointment.
5. Record the doctor's instructions.
6. Save the notes.

No major confusion was found during the test.
